How availability is worked out

Every availability check counts active holds

When ServiceNow works out which slots to return, it starts from slot capacity, removes confirmed bookings, then removes every hold still inside its 10 minutes. The same count is used for Shopify shoppers and for staff booking in ServiceNow. A slot that reaches zero is not sent to Shopify, so no other shopper can see or select it.

Design rules

Four rules the flow relies on

Ownership

ServiceNow owns the hold

The hold sits next to the capacity data, so "is it free" and "hold it" happen in one call. The middleware orchestrates and never caches availability.

Capacity

Holds use up capacity

Available = slot capacity − booked appointments − active holds. Active means state = held and expires_at > now.

Expiry

Expiry is checked twice

Availability ignores an expired hold straight away. A job every minute then marks it released for cleanup and reporting.

Retries

Keyed on Shopify IDs

Holds key on cart token + line ID, confirmations on Shopify order ID. A retried webhook never makes a second hold or work order.

Open decision

Do manual bookings in ServiceNow need a hold?

Online bookings always hold the slot for 10 minutes. We have not decided whether staff booking a phone or walk-in customer in ServiceNow should also hold the slot while they collect customer and vehicle details. Pick an option, then switch between its happy route and alternate route.
